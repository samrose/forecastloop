# Scenarios

## Steady labor market in mid-Michigan

Domain: regional_economy

The Lansing-area economy (state government, Michigan State University, health care and auto
manufacturing) keeps unemployment close to its 2024 to 2026 range. Housing construction in
Michigan stays near its recent level.

- The Ingham County unemployment rate stays at or below 5% for the last month of each quarter.
- Michigan building permits in each quarter stay within 15% of the same quarter one year earlier.

## Manufacturing slowdown

Domain: regional_economy

A downturn in auto manufacturing reduces hiring in mid-Michigan. Unemployment rises over two
or more quarters, and new housing permits fall below the prior year.

- The Ingham County unemployment rate rises by more than 1 percentage point over 3 months.
- Michigan building permits in a quarter fall below the same quarter one year earlier.
